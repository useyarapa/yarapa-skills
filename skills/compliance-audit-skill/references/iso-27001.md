# ISO/IEC 27001 Audit Profile

Baseline: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 plus Amendment 1:2024, subject to live version verification.

## Scope gate

Do not assess organization-wide conformity until these are known:

- ISMS scope and boundaries;
- organizational context and relevant interested parties;
- information assets/processes/services covered;
- risk-assessment and risk-treatment method;
- applicable legal/contractual requirements;
- Statement of Applicability (SoA) or equivalent control-applicability record;
- evidence period.

If missing, perform a limited readiness or technical-control review only.

## Management-system evidence areas

Assess evidence for the management-system lifecycle, including:

- context, scope, interfaces, and interested parties;
- leadership/accountability and policy;
- risk/opportunity planning;
- information-security risk assessment and treatment;
- objectives and planning to achieve them;
- resources, competence, awareness, communication, documented information;
- operational planning and control;
- risk-assessment/treatment operation;
- monitoring, measurement, analysis, evaluation;
- internal audit;
- management review;
- nonconformity, corrective action, and continual improvement.

The 2024 amendment adds climate-action considerations to the management-system context clauses. Verify the amended official text from ISO or the user's licensed copy; do not invent the exact wording.

## Annex A / control audit method

Do not paste the copyrighted Annex A control text. Use the organization’s licensed standard and SoA as the control source.

For each applicable control:

1. record control identifier;
2. record applicability and SoA rationale;
3. identify policy/process owner;
4. inspect design evidence;
5. inspect technical/operational implementation evidence;
6. sample operating evidence where recurrence is required;
7. record exceptions and compensating arrangements;
8. classify finding state.

Group analysis by the 2022 control themes when useful:

- organizational;
- people;
- physical;
- technological.

## High-value evidence examples

- ISMS scope statement;
- risk methodology, risk register, treatment plan;
- SoA;
- security objectives and metrics;
- asset/service inventories and ownership;
- IAM/MFA/PAM/service-account evidence;
- supplier/security due diligence and agreements;
- secure development and change controls;
- vulnerability/patch evidence;
- logging/monitoring/incident evidence;
- backup/restore and continuity evidence;
- internal audit program/results;
- management-review records;
- corrective-action records.

## Conclusion rule

Repository-only evidence can establish specific technical facts but cannot prove full ISO/IEC 27001 conformity. Certification status requires independent certification evidence from a competent certification body, not this skill's judgment.
