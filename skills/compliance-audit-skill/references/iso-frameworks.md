# Additional ISO Framework Profiles

Use the user's licensed standard for exact normative text. This file provides scope and evidence guidance only.

## ISO/IEC 27002:2022 — Information security controls

Role: implementation guidance that supports information-security controls; not independently certifiable.

Use when:
- designing or evaluating control implementation;
- collecting evidence for an ISO/IEC 27001 SoA/control set;
- comparing implementation maturity without claiming 27002 certification.

Audit output should refer to guidance alignment, not certification.

## ISO/IEC 27701:2025 — Privacy information management

Scope gate:
- PIMS scope;
- PII controller/processor roles;
- categories of PII/data subjects;
- processing purposes and data flows;
- legal/contractual privacy obligations;
- processors/subprocessors and transfers;
- retention/deletion and data-subject request mechanisms;
- privacy risk assessment and operating evidence.

Important: 2019 is withdrawn; use 2025 as the current baseline unless a specific transition/legacy assessment is requested.

## ISO 22301:2019 + Amd 1:2024 — Business continuity

Scope gate:
- BCMS scope;
- critical products/services/processes;
- business impact analysis;
- disruption scenarios and dependencies;
- continuity strategies and plans;
- recovery objectives;
- exercise/test evidence;
- incident/continuity communications;
- management review and improvement.

Version rule: a revision is under development. Use the current published edition until ISO publishes its replacement; never use a draft as normative by default.

## ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI management system

Scope gate:
- AI management-system scope;
- AI system inventory and roles;
- intended use and affected stakeholders;
- AI risks/impacts and treatment;
- lifecycle processes and supplier dependencies;
- data/model provenance and governance evidence where applicable;
- human oversight, transparency, monitoring, incident/change handling;
- objectives, audits, management review, corrective actions.

Do not infer model safety/effectiveness from policy documents alone; verify technical and operational evidence appropriate to the AI use case.

## ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 — Service management

Scope gate:
- service management system scope;
- services/service catalog and customers;
- service requirements and SLAs;
- supplier/relationship model;
- change/release/configuration/incident/problem processes;
- availability, capacity, continuity, security interfaces;
- monitoring, internal audit, management review, improvement.

## ISO 31000:2018 — Risk management guidance

This is guidance, not a certifiable management-system requirements standard.

Evaluate:
- governance/integration of risk management;
- risk criteria and context;
- identification, analysis, evaluation and treatment;
- communication/consultation;
- monitoring/review;
- recording/reporting;
- evidence that risk treatment affects real decisions.

Report "alignment with guidance", not conformity certification.

## ISO 9001:2026 — Quality management system

Scope gate:
- QMS scope and products/services;
- customer and applicable statutory/regulatory requirements;
- process ownership/interactions;
- quality objectives/metrics;
- competence/resources;
- design/development where applicable;
- external providers;
- production/service controls;
- nonconforming outputs;
- monitoring/customer feedback;
- internal audit, management review, corrective action and improvement.

Version rule: ISO 9001:2026 replaced ISO 9001:2015 in September 2026. For existing certifications, separately verify transition arrangements with the relevant certification body/accreditation scheme.
