## Source hierarchy rule

For standards with proprietary normative text, do not reconstruct missing requirements from memory, secondary articles, or LLM knowledge. Use the official standard text available to the auditor or an authorized licensed copy. When only public information is available, restrict conclusions accordingly and mark the framework `ALIGNED`; do not claim formal conformity.
